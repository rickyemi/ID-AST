# Methods references

* CLSI. *M100: Performance Standards for Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing.* Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute. Source of the clinical breakpoints in `data/external/AST_breakpoints_reference.csv`.
* CLSI. *M07: Methods for Dilution Antimicrobial Susceptibility Tests for Bacteria That Grow Aerobically.* Broth microdilution reference method and two-fold dilution scheme.
* U.S. FDA (2009). *Class II Special Controls Guidance: Antimicrobial Susceptibility Test (AST) Systems.* Definitions of essential agreement, categorical agreement, very major, major and minor errors, and the usual acceptance levels (EA and CA ≥ 90%, VME ≤ 1.5%, ME ≤ 3%).
* ISO 20776-2:2021. *Evaluation of performance of antimicrobial susceptibility test devices against reference broth micro-dilution.*
* Chen T., Guestrin C. (2016). *XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System.* KDD.
* Cortes C., Vapnik V. (1995). *Support-vector networks.* Machine Learning 20:273–297. Platt J. (1999), probabilistic outputs for SVMs.
* Gorishniy Y. et al. (2021). *Revisiting Deep Learning Models for Tabular Data* (FT-Transformer). NeurIPS.
* Bergstra J., Bengio Y. (2012). *Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization.* JMLR 13:281–305.
* Benjamini Y., Hochberg Y. (1995). *Controlling the false discovery rate.* JRSS-B 57:289–300.
* Youden W. J. (1950). *Index for rating diagnostic tests.* Cancer 3:32–35.
* Clopper C. J., Pearson E. S. (1934). The exact binomial confidence interval used for the VME upper bound.
